Questions owners ask us

How much does it cost?

The first call is free. After it, we quote your pilot at a fixed price, so you know the full cost before we start. No monthly contract is required.

Do I have to change the software I use?

Usually not. We connect to the email, WhatsApp, spreadsheets and CRM you already have. If something would need to change, we tell you before we start.

What happens to my business data?

Your data stays in your accounts. We only get the access a task needs, and you can remove it any time.

What if my team doesn't use it?

Getting it used is part of the job. We train your team, watch how it goes in the first weeks, and adjust it until it fits how they work.
